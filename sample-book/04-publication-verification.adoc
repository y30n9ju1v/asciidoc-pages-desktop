== Chapter 4: PDF Publication Verification

This final chapter is a compact acceptance test for the print PDF pipeline.
Open `main.adoc` from the `sample-book` folder, wait for the PDF preview, and
confirm the following publishing conventions in the generated pages.

=== Numbered Listings and Callouts

The automatic reference <<lst-publisher>> must read as a numbered listing in
the PDF. Its source markers render as `(1)` through `(4)`, and each marker has
its explanation immediately below the listing.

An author-supplied label takes precedence over automatic publication wording:
see <<lst-publisher,the publisher routine>>. This must retain “the publisher
routine” rather than being replaced by the automatic listing label.

=== Numbered Equations and References

The automatic reference <<eq-sum-identity>> must read as a numbered equation
in the PDF. Its equation number appears at the right edge, while the formula
itself stays centered on the text measure.

An explicit cross-reference also keeps its editorial wording: <<eq-sum-identity,the triangular-number identity>>.

=== PDF Preview Checklist

* [x] Listing caption, `(n)` markers, and callout explanations stay together.
* [x] Equation number and optional caption use the PDF publication layout.
* [x] Automatic and explicit cross-reference labels remain distinct.
* [x] Included chapters, local JPG/PNG images, and Mermaid diagrams render in one manuscript.
